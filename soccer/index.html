For families & teachers: year plan and learning levels

Choose levels 1–5 to start a match at that level, or “Resume saved levels” to use the last level for each skill. Questions are generated one at a time. Two correct first attempts without hints raise that skill one level; the first wrong answer lowers it one level and prioritizes it on the next play. Repeated retries on the same question do not lower the level again. Levels stay between 1 and 5. At level 1, the coach’s mini-lesson opens automatically.

Levels use smaller or larger numbers, simpler or more involved tasks, and fewer choices at Warm-up. Some concept questions use difficulty bands rather than five different question types. Progress shows practice and accuracy, not a certification of mastery. Students should use paper, concrete materials, and conversation to explain their reasoning.
